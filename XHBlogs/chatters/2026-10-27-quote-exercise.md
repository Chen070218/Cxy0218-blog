The quote collection exercise looked small: one page, twenty quotes, one stylesheet. It turned out to cover most of the HTML lesson at once.

I used a real `<blockquote>` for each quote and a `<cite>` for the author, a `<ul>` for the list, and one `h1` for the page. No `div` soup. Then the styling was mostly spacing and a left border, because the structure was already doing the work.

The part that surprised me was how much better it read once the markup was semantic. Not because the browser draws it differently — it does not — but because the page suddenly had an outline I could follow, and a screen reader would get the same outline.
